# Revision rules

Two revision rounds are included.

A revision means:
- trim timing;
- caption wording;
- music swap;
- minor layout change;
- replacing a clip with another clip already supplied.

A new video means:
- new hook direction;
- new raw footage;
- new offer;
- new script;
- a different target audience.
